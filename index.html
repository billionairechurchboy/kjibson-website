§ 01 Services

Four desks.
One file.

Admission, visa, housing and flights — handled by one team that already knows your case.

University admission

We guide you from first inquiry to enrollment letter. That includes choosing the right Serbian university, preparing your application form, collecting transcripts and recommendation letters, and submitting everything on time.

Our team checks every document before it goes to admissions, so you don't get rejected over missing or incorrect paperwork.

  • University & program selection
  • Application form preparation
  • Transcripts & recommendation letters
  • Document review before submission
  • Enrollment letter

Visa processing

Once you are admitted, we handle the student visa process: we compile the embassy requirements, review your financial and academic documents, and prepare you for your appointment.

We stay in touch until approval, so you know exactly when you can travel to Serbia.

  • Embassy requirement checklist
  • Financial document review
  • Academic document review
  • Appointment preparation
  • Follow-up to approval

Accommodation

We arrange student housing or a dormitory place close to your campus in Belgrade or another university city, matched to your budget, program length and preferred living setup.

You arrive with a confirmed place to stay instead of searching on your own after landing.

  • Dormitory placement
  • Private student housing
  • Close to your campus
  • Matched to your budget
  • Confirmed before you fly

Flight tickets

We book your return flights to Serbia with dates aligned to your program start and visa approval, and we coordinate layovers, baggage allowances and any schedule changes if your intake date shifts.

That's one less thing to organise before you start your studies abroad.

  • Return flights to Serbia
  • Dates aligned to your visa
  • Layovers & baggage
  • Rebooking if your intake shifts
